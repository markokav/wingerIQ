# Winger IQ: scenario library

All 5 scenarios, each in 2 versions (10 decision pictures). Generated from the game code, so it matches what players see.
In every pair, the starting picture is the same and one situation changes (position, shout, body language), which flips the best play.

This file doubles as the **coach review sheet**: each version ends with review boxes for a licensed youth coach.

## 1. Wide 1v1: Overlap coming

### Version 1: #2 is free

**Situation:** 70th minute, 0–0. Your #8 is about to pass it out wide to you. (minute 70, score 0–0)

**What the player can see and hear:**
- Shout: #8: "Time! Turn!"
- Shout: #2: "Over! Over!"
- Their left-back stopped about 4 m away: low, side-on, inside foot forward. He is set, and he is showing you down the line.
- Your #2 sprints past on the outside. Nobody follows him.
- Their #6 has moved across to block the way inside.
- Defender read-out in replay: Showing you outside – Set: stopped, low, side-on, inside foot forward. He wants you down the line.

**Coach's eye hint (Learn mode):** Coach’s eye: He’s stopped, low and side-on, showing you down the line. Who else is free?

| Option | Rating | Result | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| ➜ Pass to #2 down the line | Best | Goal | The left-back was busy with you. That left your #2 totally free. |
| ↖ Cut inside and shoot | Poor | Blocked | Two defenders blocked the inside. Your #2 was free outside. |
| ↗ Dribble down the line | Poor | Lost it | He was showing you down the line, and you went exactly where he wanted, into your #2’s lane. |
| ↩ Back to #8 | Okay | Ball kept | Safe, but you gave away a 2v1 your team worked hard for. |

**Too slow (Pro mode):** plays out like "↗ Dribble down the line" and is rated Poor.

**Rule shown:** Overlap + nobody tracking = 2v1. Release the ball early!

**"But if…":** But if their winger had tracked #2, cutting inside or keeping the ball would be smarter.

## 3. Back to goal: Man on!

### Version 1: tight on your back

**Situation:** 60th minute, 0–0. You've come short to get the ball from your #2. Their left-back is close. (minute 60, score 0–0)

**What the player can see and hear:**
- Shout: #2: "Man on!"
- Their left-back is right behind you. There is no time to turn.
- Your #2 is free just behind the ball. Perfect for a first-time pass.
- Their #11 is too far away to stop #2 playing it back over the top.
- Defender read-out in replay: Tight on your back – Inside the tackle zone. No time to turn: play it off first time.

**Coach's eye hint (Learn mode):** Coach’s eye: He’s right on your back. Can you turn, or should you play it off?

| 🛡 Shield it | Okay | Throw-in | You kept it, just. But their #11 came to double up and the attack died. |

**Rule shown:** Defender tight on your back? Don’t turn. Bounce it off and spin in behind!

## 5. 2v1 counter: Commit, then pass

### Version 1: he steps to you

**Situation:** 88th minute, 2–2. Counter-attack! You're driving through the middle with your #9 alongside. One defender is left. (minute 88, score 2–2)

**What the player can see and hear:**
- Shout: #9: "Yes! Square!"
- The last defender stepped towards you. He can’t mark two players at once.
- Your #9 is free on your left, level with you.
- Their midfielders are sprinting back. You have about two seconds.
- Defender read-out in replay: Stepping to you – He has committed to you, so he can’t also mark your #9.

**Coach's eye hint (Learn mode):** Coach’s eye: The last defender is stepping towards you. Who does that leave free?

| Option | Rating | Result | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| ➜ Pass to #9 | Best | Goal | The defender stepped to you. That left #9 alone, so the simple pass won it. |
| 🎯 Shoot from here | Okay | Corner | A shot from 20 metres gave the keeper an easy save. #9 was free for a tap-in. |
| ↗ Keep driving at goal | Poor | Tackled | Dribbling into the last defender gave him the chance to tackle. #9 was free. |
| ⏸ Slow down and wait | Poor | Chance gone | Waiting let their defenders sprint back. The 2v1 became 2v3. |

**Too slow (Pro mode):** plays out like "⏸ Slow down and wait" and is rated Poor.

**Rule shown:** 2v1? Run at the defender. When he steps to you, pass!

**"But if…":** But if the defender had stayed with #9, keep driving and shoot yourself.
